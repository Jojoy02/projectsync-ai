# Safety and writeback rules

1. Source access must be authorized before reading.
2. Do not infer task completion from a calendar event or a meeting invitation.
3. Do not claim ownership of teammates' work.
4. Do not merge similarly named projects without supporting evidence.
5. Do not auto-publish drafts.
6. Confirm daily and weekly drafts independently.
7. Re-read the target section before writeback and compare with the review snapshot.
8. If the target changed, is ambiguous, or the API reports a conflict, stop instead of overwriting.
9. Never claim success before the document tool confirms success.
10. Keep secrets and personal facts out of public repositories and logs.
